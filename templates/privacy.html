Privacy

How Print Me Maybe uses the details you give us when you order.

What we collect

At checkout we ask for your name, email, and — for delivery — phone number and address. Order notes are optional (colour, a name to print, a file description). We also keep a signed cart cookie on your device for about seven days so your basket survives a refresh.

Why

We use those details to pack your order, arrange pick up or courier delivery in Cyprus or Greece, and email you a confirmation and a private order link. We do not create customer accounts.

Payments and email

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Card numbers never touch this website. Order emails are sent with Resend from orders@print-me-maybe.com.

How long

We keep order records (including the address and notes you typed) so we can fulfil, refund, and answer questions. Listing photos and the catalog live on this server. We do not sell your details.

Contact

Questions: @print.me.maybe or the email on your order confirmation.
